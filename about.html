Our Story

About ROSE Tech Academy

Opening pathways into technology careers. ROSE Tech Academy helps learners move confidently into high-demand tech roles by reducing barriers, simplifying the learning journey, and focusing on practical training, clear pathways, and employer-valued skills.

Our Vision

Vision Statement

To help every learner rise into in-demand technology careers through accessible, job-ready training, delivered with the warmth, dignity, and courage that Rosie lived by.

Mission

The R-O-S-E values

R — Resilient Learning

Practical, confidence-building courses for real jobs.

O — Opportunity for All

Affordable, flexible, inclusive—scholarships where possible.

S — Skills that Pay

Employer-validated skills, certifications, and career pathways.

E — Ethical Impact

Transparent outcomes, learner support, and give-back in Rosie's honor.

ROSE Tech Academy Founding Team

Education & Technology Strategy

Our founding team brings together backgrounds in technology training, career coaching, and curriculum design. United by a shared frustration with confusing, fragmented tech education, we built ROSE as the resource we wished had existed when we were starting out.
