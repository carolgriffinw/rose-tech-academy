Privacy Policy

We take your privacy seriously. This policy explains what information we collect and how it's used.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 1, 2026

Information We Collect

ROSE Tech Academy collects limited personal information to operate our website and services effectively. This includes information you voluntarily provide, such as your name and email address when subscribing to our newsletter or submitting a contact form, as well as automatically collected technical information like browser type, device type, and general usage patterns collected through standard analytics tools.

How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to respond to inquiries, send newsletter communications you've opted into, improve our website content and learning path recommendations, and monitor site performance and security. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Cookies

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to remember your preferences (such as dark/light mode), understand how visitors use our site, and support basic site functionality. You can control cookie behavior through your browser settings, though disabling cookies may affect certain site features.

Third-Party Links

Our site contains links to third-party education platforms and certification providers, including Microsoft Learn, Coursera, edX, Udemy, and others. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of these third-party sites. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any external site you visit.

Affiliate Disclosure

ROSE Tech Academy participates in affiliate marketing programs. This means we may earn a commission when you click certain links and make a purchase or enroll in a course, at no additional cost to you. For full details, see our Affiliate Disclosure page.

Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect the personal information we collect from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of your personal information. To exercise any of these rights, or if you have questions about how your data is handled, please contact us using the information below.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, please reach out via our Contact page or email us directly at hello@rosetechacademy.com.
