AWS vs Azure vs Google Cloud: A Beginner's Comparison

Cloud Computing ROSE Tech Academy · 2025-04-15 · 9 min read

Amazon Web Services (AWS), Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud Platform (GCP) together account for more than 65% of global cloud infrastructure spending. All three are excellent platforms, all three are actively hiring, and skills on one transfer meaningfully to the others. But when you are starting out, picking one to focus on is important — and the right choice depends on your career goals, your employer, and the market near you.

Market Share and Job Availability

AWS holds the largest share of the cloud market — approximately 31% as of late 2024, according to Synergy Research Group. Azure is second at around 25%, and Google Cloud is third at around 11%. This matters for job seekers because AWS has the largest pool of job openings, but Azure has the fastest growth in enterprise adoption, particularly in organisations already using Microsoft 365.

Check the job boards in your city or region. In some markets (government, healthcare, financial services), Azure dominates. In startups and technology companies, AWS is more common. Google Cloud is strongest in media, gaming, and data-intensive industries.

AWS: The Market Leader

AWS launched in 2006 and has been the dominant cloud provider ever since. Its strengths:

  • Largest service catalogue — over 200 services
  • Strongest presence in startups and technology companies
  • Most certifications and the most mature certification path
  • Widest global region coverage
  • Strong free tier for learning and experimentation

AWS certifications are widely recognised and valued by recruiters. The AWS Cloud Practitioner is the best starting point, followed by Solutions Architect Associate — the most popular associate-level cloud certification in the world.

Microsoft Azure: The Enterprise Choice

Azure is the preferred choice for organisations already invested in Microsoft technologies — Microsoft 365, Active Directory, Dynamics 365, and Visual Studio. Azure's strengths:

  • Deep integration with Microsoft enterprise software
  • Strong hybrid cloud capabilities (Azure Arc, Azure Stack)
  • Preferred by regulated industries (finance, healthcare, government)
  • Excellent AI and data services (Azure OpenAI, Fabric, Synapse)
  • Free Microsoft Learn modules for every certification

If you work for an organisation that uses Microsoft products, Azure is likely already deployed somewhere in the environment. Starting with AZ-900 is a natural fit.

Google Cloud: The Data and AI Specialist

Google Cloud is where the company's extraordinary strengths in data, AI, and machine learning shine. BigQuery — Google's serverless data warehouse — is considered best-in-class. Google's AI research has produced foundational models like BERT and Transformer, and Vertex AI is a leading MLOps platform.

  • Best-in-class data warehousing (BigQuery)
  • Strongest AI/ML research and tools
  • Kubernetes (originally developed at Google)
  • Strong networking capabilities (Andromeda, custom silicon)
  • Growing enterprise adoption, especially in analytics-heavy industries
🗺️ Quick Decision Guide

Choose AWS if you want the most job opportunities and are targeting startups or tech companies. Choose Azure if your employer uses Microsoft products or you are targeting enterprise or government roles. Choose Google Cloud if you are targeting data engineering, machine learning, or media/gaming companies.

What About Multi-Cloud?

Many enterprises use multiple cloud providers simultaneously. As you advance in your career, multi-cloud skills become increasingly valuable. But start with one — the underlying concepts (virtualisation, networking, IAM, storage) are the same across all three. Once you understand them deeply on one platform, adapting to another takes weeks, not months.

Certification Comparison

  • Entry-level: AWS Cloud Practitioner / Azure AZ-900 / Google Cloud Digital Leader
  • Associate: AWS Solutions Architect Associate / Azure AZ-104 / Google Associate Cloud Engineer
  • Professional: AWS Solutions Architect Professional / Azure AZ-305 / Google Professional Cloud Architect
