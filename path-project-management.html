Learning Path

Project Management

Develop the planning, delivery, Agile, Scrum, and leadership skills that project managers need across every industry.

Why Project Management

Technology projects succeed or fail based on how well they're planned, coordinated, and delivered—which is why skilled project managers are consistently in demand, both within technology teams and across the broader business world.

This path covers traditional project management fundamentals alongside Agile and Scrum methodologies, preparing you for the certifications that are most respected by hiring managers, including the PMP.

Skills You'll Gain

  • Project Planning
  • Agile & Scrum
  • Stakeholder Management
  • Risk Management
  • Budgeting & Scheduling
  • Team Leadership
