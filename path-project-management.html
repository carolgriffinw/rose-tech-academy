Learning Path

Project Management

Develop the planning, delivery, Agile, Scrum, and leadership skills to manage projects in any industry.

Why Choose Project Management

Technology projects rely on strong planning, coordination, and execution, creating steady demand for skilled project managers across technology teams and industries.

Builds foundational project management knowledge, practical experience, and leadership skills.

Develops skills in predictive methods, Agile principles, and business analysis while validating your ability to lead projects and manage people, processes, and business priorities across predictive, adaptive, Agile, and hybrid approaches. It also builds the confidence needed to pursue advanced certifications.

This path combines essential project management practices with Agile and Scrum methods to prepare you for employer-valued certifications, including the PMP.

Skills You’ll Build

  • Project Planning
  • Agile & Scrum
  • Stakeholder Management
  • Risk Management
  • Budgeting & Scheduling
  • Team Leadership
