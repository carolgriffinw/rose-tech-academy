Accessibility Statement

Last updated: June 1, 2026

Our Commitment

ROSE Tech Academy is committed to ensuring digital accessibility for people of all abilities. We continually work to improve the usability and accessibility of our website, believing that technology education should be available to everyone.

Standards

We aim to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA. These guidelines explain how to make web content more accessible for people with disabilities and more usable for everyone.

Accessibility Features

Our website includes several features to support accessibility, including semantic HTML structure, keyboard-navigable menus and forms, sufficient color contrast between text and backgrounds, descriptive alt text and ARIA labels for interactive elements, and support for both light and dark display modes.

Known Issues

While we strive for full accessibility, some areas of our site may not yet fully conform to all WCAG 2.1 AA criteria. We are actively working to identify and remediate these issues as part of our ongoing accessibility efforts.

Feedback

We welcome feedback on the accessibility of the ROSE Tech Academy website. If you encounter any barriers or have suggestions for improvement, please let us know so we can address them.

Contact

To report an accessibility issue or request assistance, please reach out via our Contact page or email hello@rosetechacademy.com. We aim to respond to accessibility-related inquiries promptly.
