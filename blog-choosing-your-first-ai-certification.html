Choosing Your First AI Certification

Artificial Intelligence ROSE Tech Academy · 7 min read

Artificial intelligence is no longer a niche specialty — it's becoming a core literacy expected across technology, business, marketing, and operations roles. If you're looking to validate your AI knowledge, the three major cloud providers each offer entry-level AI certifications. But which one should you pursue first?

The AI Certification Landscape

The entry-level AI certification space is dominated by three credentials:

  • Microsoft Azure AI Fundamentals (AI-900)
  • Google AI Essentials (non-technical) or Google Cloud Digital Leader
  • AWS Certified AI Practitioner

All three are designed for beginners — no programming or math prerequisites are required. They focus on concepts, use cases, and the vendor's own AI services rather than hands-on model building.

Microsoft AI-900: Azure AI Fundamentals

The AI-900 is widely regarded as the most approachable entry-level AI certification. Microsoft has invested heavily in AI education through its Copilot and Azure OpenAI products, and the exam reflects a broad, conceptual curriculum.

  • Cost: ~$165
  • Study time: 3–6 weeks
  • Format: ~60 multiple-choice questions, 60-minute exam
  • Best for: Business analysts, project managers, non-developers entering AI

Tip: Microsoft offers free AI-900 preparation on Microsoft Learn, including interactive modules and practice assessments. It's one of the best free study paths available for any cloud certification.

Google AI Essentials / Cloud Digital Leader

Google offers two relevant entry-level options. The Google AI Essentials course (Coursera) is a non-proctored certificate focused on using AI tools in the workplace. The Cloud Digital Leader exam is a formal proctored certification covering Google Cloud products including AI services.

  • Cost: ~$200 for Cloud Digital Leader; AI Essentials is part of a Coursera subscription
  • Best for: Those interested in Google Cloud or already in a Google Workspace environment

AWS Certified AI Practitioner

Amazon launched the AWS Certified AI Practitioner credential in 2024, targeting professionals who want to understand AI/ML concepts within the AWS ecosystem. It covers Amazon Bedrock, SageMaker, and responsible AI principles.

  • Cost: ~$150
  • Study time: 4–8 weeks
  • Best for: Those already using AWS or targeting AWS-heavy employers

Which Should You Take First?

The answer depends on your situation:

  • If you work with Microsoft products (Office 365, Azure, Teams) → Start with AI-900.
  • If you're targeting cloud roles at AWS-heavy companies → AWS AI Practitioner makes sense alongside Cloud Practitioner.
  • If you're focused on data or analytics → Google Cloud Digital Leader pairs well with a data analytics path.

For most beginners, Microsoft AI-900 is the safest first choice: the free study materials are excellent, the exam is well-structured, and Microsoft's AI investments make the certification increasingly relevant.

What Comes After?

Once you've passed an entry-level AI cert, consider advancing to applied AI skills: prompt engineering, working with language models, building AI-powered workflows, or pursuing the Generative AI learning path for more depth.
