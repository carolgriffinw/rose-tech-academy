Choosing Your First AI Certification

Artificial Intelligence ROSE Tech Academy · 2025-02-15 · 7 min read

Artificial intelligence certifications have proliferated in the past two years as every major cloud provider races to capitalise on the AI boom. For beginners, this abundance of choice creates a genuine problem: which certification should you start with, and will it actually lead somewhere?

This guide compares the three most accessible entry-level AI certifications — Microsoft AI-900, Google Cloud Digital Leader (AI track), and AWS Certified AI Practitioner — to help you make an informed choice.

Microsoft Azure AI Fundamentals (AI-900)

AI-900 is Microsoft's entry-level AI certification. It covers foundational AI and machine learning concepts, Azure AI services (Computer Vision, Language, Speech, and Decision), and responsible AI principles. It requires no coding experience and typically takes three to five weeks to prepare with Microsoft's free Learn modules.

  • Best for: Professionals already in the Microsoft ecosystem, beginners with no technical background, business analysts exploring AI
  • Exam cost: $165 USD
  • Time to prepare: 3–5 weeks part-time
  • Difficulty: Beginner (no coding required)
  • Next certification: AI-102 (Azure AI Engineer Associate)

AWS Certified AI Practitioner (AIF-C01)

Launched in late 2024, AWS Certified AI Practitioner is Amazon's response to growing AI certification demand. It covers foundational AI/ML concepts, generative AI fundamentals, responsible AI, and AWS AI/ML services including Bedrock, SageMaker, and Comprehend. Like AI-900, it requires no hands-on coding experience.

  • Best for: Professionals in AWS environments, those interested in generative AI on the leading cloud platform
  • Exam cost: $100 USD
  • Time to prepare: 4–6 weeks part-time
  • Difficulty: Beginner
  • Next certification: AWS Certified Machine Learning Specialty

Google Cloud Digital Leader (AI/ML Focus)

Google Cloud Digital Leader covers cloud concepts broadly, but the AI and ML modules are substantial given Google's leadership in AI research. Google's free learning path on Cloud Skills Boost makes this an accessible starting point for beginners. Note that Google also offers a separate Cloud AI Engineer certification at the associate level.

  • Best for: Those interested in Google Cloud, data science, or working at organisations that use Google Workspace
  • Exam cost: $200 USD
  • Time to prepare: 4–8 weeks part-time
  • Difficulty: Beginner to intermediate
  • Next certification: Google Professional ML Engineer
📊 Side-by-Side Summary

AI-900 wins on free prep resources and integration with other Microsoft certifications. AWS AIF-C01 is the most affordable. Google Digital Leader is best if you are already in a Google Cloud environment or targeting data-heavy roles.

Which Should You Take First?

The answer depends on your context:

  • If your employer uses Microsoft 365 or Azure, start with AI-900.
  • If you work with AWS or target large enterprises, start with AWS AI Practitioner.
  • If you are targeting data science or ML engineering, start with Google Cloud Digital Leader and then pursue Google's Professional ML Engineer.
  • If you genuinely have no preference, AI-900 has the best free study resources and a clearer onward path (AI-102, AZ-104, DP-100).

Beyond the Entry Level

All three entry-level certifications are stepping stones, not destinations. After earning one, chart a path toward associate or professional-level credentials:

  • Azure: AI-102 (Azure AI Engineer), DP-100 (Azure Data Scientist Associate)
  • AWS: Machine Learning Specialty, Data Engineer Associate
  • Google: Professional ML Engineer, Professional Data Engineer
