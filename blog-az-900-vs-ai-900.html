AZ-900 vs AI-900: Which Should You Take First?

Certification ROSE Tech Academy · 2025-04-01 · 5 min read

AZ-900 (Azure Fundamentals) and AI-900 (Azure AI Fundamentals) are Microsoft's two most popular entry-level certifications. Both are beginner-friendly, require no prior technical experience, and cost $165 USD. But they cover different territory, and the order in which you pursue them matters for your career goals.

What AZ-900 Covers

AZ-900 is the foundational Microsoft certification for cloud computing. It covers:

  • Core cloud concepts: IaaS, PaaS, SaaS, public/private/hybrid cloud models
  • Azure architecture and services: compute, networking, storage, databases
  • Azure management tools and governance
  • Azure pricing, cost management, and service level agreements
  • Azure security, compliance, privacy, and trust

AZ-900 is the prerequisite mindset (though not a formal requirement) for the entire Azure certification path. It builds the vocabulary and conceptual framework that every subsequent Azure certification assumes you have.

What AI-900 Covers

AI-900 focuses specifically on artificial intelligence and machine learning on Azure. It covers:

  • Fundamental AI concepts: machine learning, computer vision, NLP, knowledge mining, generative AI
  • Azure AI services: Azure AI Vision, Azure AI Language, Azure AI Speech, Azure OpenAI Service
  • Responsible AI principles (fairness, reliability, privacy, inclusiveness, transparency, accountability)

AI-900 does not require coding. It is conceptual and service-awareness-focused, making it accessible to both technical and non-technical learners.

Key Differences

  • AZ-900 is broader — it covers all of Azure, not just AI services. It is the better first certification if you are uncertain about your specialisation.
  • AI-900 is narrower but deeper on its topic. It is the right choice if you already know you want to work in AI or data.
  • AZ-900 is the gateway to the Azure Administrator (AZ-104), Azure Developer (AZ-204), and Azure Solutions Architect (AZ-305) paths.
  • AI-900 leads to AI-102 (Azure AI Engineer Associate) and DP-100 (Azure Data Scientist Associate).
🎯 Our Recommendation

Take AZ-900 first unless you are specifically targeting an AI career. The cloud fundamentals you learn in AZ-900 make AI-900 significantly easier to understand — Azure AI services run on the same infrastructure AZ-900 describes. If you take them in sequence, allow two to three weeks between exams.

Study Resources Comparison

  • Both exams have free, official learning paths on Microsoft Learn (learn.microsoft.com)
  • John Savill's Azure Masterclass (YouTube) is the best free AZ-900 resource
  • Microsoft's AI-900 study guide is thorough and freely available on Learn
  • Practice exams: MeasureUp, Whizlabs, and Udemy's practice test packs are popular for both

What Comes Next?

After AZ-900 and AI-900, you have several clear paths:

  • Cloud Administration: AZ-104 → AZ-305
  • AI Engineering: AI-102 → DP-100
  • Data Engineering: DP-900 → DP-203
  • Security: AZ-500 → SC-200
