AZ-900 vs AI-900: Which Should You Take First?

Certification ROSE Tech Academy · 5 min read

Microsoft offers two popular entry-level certifications for beginners: AZ-900 (Azure Fundamentals) and AI-900 (Azure AI Fundamentals). Both are foundational, both are achievable without prior technical experience, and both are recognized by employers. But which one should you tackle first?

Quick Overview

Here's how the two exams compare at a glance:

  • AZ-900: Cloud concepts, Azure services, pricing, governance, compliance, security
  • AI-900: AI and ML concepts, Azure AI services (Cognitive Services, Bot Service, Language, Vision), responsible AI
  • Both cost approximately $165 USD
  • Both can be passed with 4–8 weeks of study using free Microsoft Learn content

AZ-900: Azure Fundamentals

AZ-900 is the broadest Microsoft fundamentals certification. It covers cloud concepts that apply across all platforms — not just Azure. You'll learn about:

  • IaaS, PaaS, SaaS models
  • Core Azure compute, storage, and networking services
  • Azure pricing, billing, and cost management
  • Azure security, compliance, and identity basics
  • Azure governance tools (Policy, Blueprints, Resource locks)

AZ-900 is the better first certification for anyone who wants to work in cloud infrastructure, IT, or DevOps. It's also a prerequisite mindset for almost every other Azure certification — even if it's not formally required.

Note: AZ-900 and AI-900 have overlapping content in the identity, security, and compliance sections. Passing AZ-900 first gives you a head start on AI-900 studying.

AI-900: Azure AI Fundamentals

AI-900 focuses specifically on artificial intelligence and machine learning concepts within the Azure ecosystem. It's designed for professionals who work alongside AI systems or want to understand AI capabilities without building models themselves.

Topics include:

  • Core machine learning concepts (supervised/unsupervised learning, model training)
  • Azure Machine Learning service overview
  • Azure Cognitive Services: Vision, Language, Speech, Decision
  • Azure OpenAI Service
  • Responsible AI principles: fairness, reliability, privacy, inclusiveness, transparency, accountability

AI-900 is the right first choice for business analysts, product managers, or non-technical professionals who specifically need AI literacy.

Where They Overlap

Both exams touch on Azure's identity and security services. If you study for AZ-900 first, a portion of that content transfers directly to AI-900. Many candidates report that AI-900 feels easier after passing AZ-900 because the cloud fundamentals are already solid.

Our Recommendation

For most learners: take AZ-900 first.

Here's why: Cloud fundamentals are broader and more versatile. AZ-900 knowledge transfers to Azure security certs, data certs, and developer certs. It opens more career doors. If you then want AI-specific knowledge, AI-900 becomes easier because your cloud foundation is established.

However, if your sole focus is on AI/ML roles, business analysis, or you're already a non-technical professional who just needs AI literacy — AI-900 standalone is perfectly valid.
